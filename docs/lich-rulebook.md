# Lich Rulebook

Soul Extractor

## True, False, and False Adventure: Two Truths and One Lie

- Everyone in the adventuring party summons the dice spirit with:

```text
@LichDiceBot /roll
```

The player with the highest result becomes the host adventurer for this round.

- The host adventurer chooses one companion and grants them the title "Puzzle Master". That player will create a "two truths and one lie about myself" puzzle.

- The Puzzle Master posts a normal, non-anonymous poll with three statements: two true and one false, with fate thoroughly muddled.

For example:

1. I was tricked by a cat when I was little
2. I have attended three weddings
3. I am actually a Pisces

- Everyone gathers around the campfire, guesses, questions, and chooses the statement they believe is the "fake".

- Players may freely speak, think, and debate:

```text
You were tricked by a cat? Was it a magic cat?
How could Pisces possibly fool Shuanghua!
```

- After everyone votes, the Puzzle Master also votes, then reveals the correct answer.

- Then everyone discusses the truth and their mistaken assumptions:

```text
So the fake one was the wedding? I thought it was the cat!
I see you in a completely new light now, Frost Knight.
```

- Finally, the host adventurer announces:

```text
The next adventure begins now!
```

Then roll again:

```text
@LichDiceBot /roll
```

Tip: use a normal poll. Otherwise the lich cannot see who chose incorrectly and laugh at them.

Lich quote: "It seems the person you love does not understand you very well."

## Dark Story Collection: Lich's Riddle Night

- This psychological labyrinth is led by the undead host, Lich, who guides the adventurers into a strange narrative world.

- At the start of each round, the lich posts a story card containing an eerie ending:

```text
The Special Restaurant

A little girl ordered a lizard steak at a special restaurant.
After taking her first bite, she immediately got up and rushed toward a moving truck.
```

- Players may ask questions freely. The lich may only answer: yes / no / this is not important.

- Questions must focus on the story itself, not emotional categories.

Unsuitable question:

```text
Is this a supernatural story?
```

Suitable question:

```text
Had she experienced a disaster before?
```

- If the players guess the truth, the lich reveals the complete version of the story.

Example truth:

She once survived a cruise ship accident and ate the "lizard meat" given to her by a companion. When she tasted that flavor again, she realized that what she ate back then was not lizard meat, but the flesh of a corpse washed onto the island.

After the shock, take a deep breath: your taste buds may not be reliable either.

Lich quote: "The story is not dark. You are simply too innocent."

## Soul Riddle Theater: Twenty Questions

- In this game, any player may create the puzzle. The lich only observes.

- The puzzle giver proposes a short sentence, for example:

```text
He killed me, and she cried.
```

- The others begin asking questions and gradually piece together the truth.

- Each turn may contain only one question, and answers are limited to: yes / no / this is not important.

- There are only 20 questions in total. If the players fail to solve it, the puzzle giver wins.

- If someone guesses correctly, they win immediately while everyone else applauds or shouts:

```text
How did you guess that!?
```

- Example solution:

Prompt:

```text
He killed me, and she cried.
```

Answer:

```text
I am an onion.
```

The knife killed me, and the cook cried while cutting me.

Lich quote: "Can that little bit of logic in your head survive 20 questions?"
