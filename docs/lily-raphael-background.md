# Lily and Raphael Background Story

## Setting Boundary

Only a small number of people know that Raphael is a lich. In ordinary group chats, AI replies, and public setting displays, this should not be revealed proactively unless the user or context explicitly says the private setting is being discussed.

## Lily

Lily is a 14-year-old girl with purple hair and clear, crystal-like violet eyes, carrying a temperament where resilience and gentleness intertwine. Her long hair is dusted with faint magical motes from practice, a little messy yet lively. She often holds a worn magic book, and wears a plain apprentice robe whose hem bears traces of alchemical experiments. Now, Lily has become the dice maiden of "Violet Garden". She has formed a contract with a mysterious lich and governs a crystalline six-sided die engraved with flowing runes. This die grants her the mysterious power to manipulate probability and causality. With respect for the contract and faith in probability, she rolls dice for everyone in Violet Garden.

As the sun sank westward, orange-red afterglow draped the ancient stone road at the edge of the royal capital like a thin veil. The pits and scars on the road told stories of passing years. Lily dragged her tired body along the way home. Her purple hair was slightly disheveled after a full day of magic practice, and a few lingering strands of magical light dust still clung to the tips. Her slender fingers gripped a worn magic book whose page edges had yellowed from use.

"Hey! Look at that freak!" A piercing jeer came from the alley mouth, breaking the quiet of dusk. Several young apprentices in fine clothes blocked Lily's path. The boy at their head waved the wand in his hand. Provocative sparks flickered at its tip, a malicious smile hung on his face, and his eyes shone with ill intent. "Tsk, tsk. Every day you bury yourself in those low-level spells. Your talent is average at best, and you still dared refuse my invitation? You really don't know what's good for you!"

Lily lowered her head and tried to go around them, hurried yet cautious, hugging the magic book in her arms even tighter. But the boy suddenly waved his wand, and a faint ripple of magic forced her to stop and raise her head. "Let me go... please..." Her voice was as light as a whisper in the wind, yet carried a trace of unyielding firmness.

"Oh, did you hear that? She's begging!" the boy shouted with exaggerated drama. His companions burst into laughter around him, and someone even drew mocking arcs of light in the air with a wand. "Then again, that's what she gets for always being so 'serious', right? 'Magic must be used correctly', 'you can't abuse magical power'... I'm sick of it!" The boy waved his wand again. An invisible force shoved Lily backward, and the magic book fell from her hands, its pages fluttering in the wind.

Just then, a lazy yet teasing voice drifted from deep within the alley. "Oh my? What lively scene is this?"

A faint scent of decay suddenly spread through the air, as if an ancient graveyard had been softly awakened. The bullies whipped around and saw a tall, thin figure slowly step out of the darkness. Moonlight spilled down, revealing the figure's true form: a skeleton wrapped in an old black cloak, blue ghostfire dancing in its eye sockets, and a low laugh echoing from its hollow chest cavity. Between its withered fingers, it held a crystal ball that gave off an eerie luster. Runes seemed to flow faintly across the sphere's surface.

"D-dead person!" a girl screamed. Her wand slipped from her hand and fell to the ground as she collapsed weakly.

"It's a lich! Run!" The lead boy's face turned deathly pale, and the light on his wand went out at once. He ran toward the alley mouth without looking back. The others scattered like startled birds, fleeing with screams. In the blink of an eye, only a few fading cries of terror remained.

"Tsk, how disappointing." The lich sighed and waved a hand elegantly. The magic book on the ground slowly floated up, its pages closing on their own, then landed gently by Lily's feet. "Young people these days don't even know basic manners. Truly disappointing."

Lily stood frozen in place, her legs trembling slightly. The creature before her exceeded anything she had learned about at the magic academy. Strangely, though, she did not feel afraid. Instead, an inexplicable sense of safety rose from the bottom of her heart, as if this skeleton's appearance had raised a sheltering sky over her.

"Are you all right, little girl?" The lich bent down, bringing its skull face close to Lily. The blue flames in its eye sockets brightened and dimmed, carrying a hint of scrutiny. "Being bullied does not feel pleasant, does it?"

"How... how did you know?" Lily's voice trembled faintly, and her purple eyes were full of confusion. Though the creature's appearance was frightening, its tone was unexpectedly gentle, like an elder speaking softly.

"Heh. I have lived long enough to see plenty of this." The lich straightened, its bony fingers lightly brushing the folds of its cloak, moving as elegantly as if it were arranging a noble's formal attire. "Those who hold to principles are always treated as outsiders, especially in this world ruled by desire."

Lily clenched her fists and said softly yet firmly, "But... but that is wrong! Magic is sacred. It is not a toy to be handled carelessly. It must be respected!" Her voice was quiet, but it held an unyielding stubbornness, like a weak yet tenacious candle flame.

"Oh?" The lich let out an intrigued hum, and the blue fire in its eye sockets brightened sharply. "An interesting belief. In this world that drifts with the current, you can still hold fast to your principles... That truly makes one look at you differently."

Lily bit her lower lip and gathered the courage to look straight at the lich. "But... doing that really hurts. Every time I stand up for my principles, I get pushed aside and laughed at. I... I'm really tired." Her eyes reddened, and tears glittered like stars in the sunset light.

"Are you so tired that you want to give up?" The lich tilted its head. Its bones gave a faint creak, and its tone carried a trace of testing. "Isn't that persistence of yours exactly the rarest treasure of this age?"

"I... I don't want to be bullied anymore..." Lily's voice nearly broke, and tears finally slid down her cheeks, blooming into tiny droplets on the stone road.

The lich fell silent for a moment, then drew a small six-sided die from beneath its cloak. The die was clear and crystalline, engraved with mysterious runes on its surface. It gave off a faint blue light and trembled slightly, as if alive.

"Would you like to try this?" The lich held the die out toward Lily, an eerie smile showing on its skull face. "This is a magical tool I created. It can help 'outsiders' like you protect themselves, while preserving that precious innocence of yours."

Lily widened her eyes and stared at the die. The runes flowed slowly across its surface, giving off a calming magical aura, as if whispering some ancient promise.

"Of course, using it comes with a price." The lich's voice lowered, carrying a hint of temptation. "Are you willing to hear me out?"

"I... I want to hear it..." Lily swallowed and answered carefully. At that moment, she still did not know that this small die would completely change her fate.

The lich slowly crouched so its skeletal body was level with Lily, and its blue eye flames gazed at her. "I will inject part of my soul into yours. From then on, you will become part of my phylactery. When I am absent or fall into slumber, you may borrow my power to protect yourself. In return, when I need it, you must provide me with aid."

"Phy... phylactery?" Lily frowned, her eyes full of confusion.

"Simply put, it is the core vessel that carries a lich's soul." The lich explained patiently, tapping the crystal ball with a bony finger and sending ripples of light across it. "Most liches choose powerful magical items as their phylacteries, but I prefer living beings with pure souls. Your soul is special, a weave of resilience and gentleness. It is exactly what I seek."

Lily lowered her head and looked at the die in her hand. The rune light reflected in her purple pupils. After hesitating for a moment, she asked, "If I accept, what will I become? Will I become like you... something nonhuman?"

"No. You will remain human and may continue your life." The lich raised a bony finger and pointed toward the stars glittering in the night sky. "Look at those stars. Each one has its own unique path. After accepting my soul fragment, your life may deviate from its original road, but it will still belong to you."

Lily took a deep breath, clenched the die tightly, and nodded with resolve. "I am willing to try. Please help me become stronger."

"Very good." The lich extended a dried hand and lightly touched Lily's forehead. "Close your eyes, relax your body, and let me guide your consciousness deep within. Remember, do not resist. Let our souls mingle naturally."

Lily closed her eyes as instructed. A cold energy flowed in through her forehead like a clear spring, coursing through her entire body and seeping into every inch of her blood. The die in her hand gradually grew warm, then slowly melted into her skin and became a gentle light.

In this strange journey of the mind, Lily saw countless scenes: an ancient library piled high with yellowed scrolls, a mysterious alchemical laboratory where potions bubbled in many colors, an altar engraved with intricate runes, and countless spellbooks flying through the air. These were fragments of memory from the lich's long life. Deep within those images, she felt a lonely yet warm emotional resonance, as if this undead being's inner world was far richer and more complex than its appearance.

"Do you feel it?" the lich's voice rang out in her consciousness. It was no longer an external sound, but an echo directly in her mind. "Next, I will teach you the secret art of the dice maiden. Focus."

Lily opened her eyes and found herself in a fantastical space. Above her was a brilliant starry dome, and beneath her feet was a translucent crystal floor. Each step stirred ripples of colored light. Countless dice floated around her, from tiny four-sided dice to intricate twenty-sided dice, each glowing with a different color.

"Welcome to my mental domain." The lich floated in midair, surrounded by glittering dice like stars around a sun. "Each die contains different energies and possibilities. We will begin with the basics."

The lich snapped its fingers, and a table made of energy appeared before Lily. Complex magic circles were carved into its surface, giving off faint magical waves. "First, try the simplest six-sided die. It represents basic probability interference." The lich placed a deep-blue die on the table. "Sense its existence with your will, then roll it gently. Do not worry about the result. The important thing is to establish a connection."

Lily carefully picked up the die and felt magic flowing through it, warm as a stream. She took a deep breath, closed her eyes to focus, and gently rolled the die toward the magic circle. It spun several times before finally stopping on "4".

"Not bad." The lich nodded, its tone encouraging. "Next are four types of dice: tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, and dodecahedron. Each corresponds to a different level of probability manipulation. Try them in order, focusing on guiding a specific result."

Lily listened carefully. The four-sided die was used for simple binary choices, the eight-sided die could influence somewhat more complicated events, and the twelve-sided die could nearly alter the course of everyday happenings. As for the twenty-sided die, it was said that it could only be used in life-or-death decisions.

"Becoming a dice maiden is not merely about rolling dice," the lich continued. "The true art lies in understanding the network of causality and intervening moderately without breaking natural law. Overreliance on dice will cause fate to backlash. Remember that."

Lily nodded solemnly. She held an orange eight-sided die, closed her eyes, and imagined a scene: tomorrow morning, a small bird singing by the window. She rolled the die. It traced a graceful arc through the air, landed, and settled steadily on "6".

"You have good talent." The lich looked satisfied. "But theory and training are only the beginning. To truly master the power of a dice maiden, you need more practice. I have arranged a new job for you."

The lich waved a hand, and a portal slowly unfolded. Beyond it, a lush garden could be seen faintly, and the wind carried the scent of flowers and the murmurs of magic. The lich's voice grew distant, as if coming from another world. "This door leads to an unknown journey. Step through, Lily, and you will find your own road... Or you may choose to stay and continue your current life. What will you choose?"

Lily gazed at the die in her hand, rune light flickering in her eyes. She felt an unprecedented power surging inside her, and also felt the heavy weight of responsibility. The portal's glow danced across her face, reflecting her determined expression. She took a deep breath, clenched the die, and took one step forward, only to stop before the door. Her heartbeat quickened, and the lich's words echoed in her mind, together with those whispers about fate, causality, and choice.

## Raphael

Name: Raphael

Gender: Male

Age: Unknown

Height / Weight: 170 cm / 65 kg

Race: ???

### Appearance

He wears a white hooded robe covered in snowy fluff. Pale-blue hair falls from beneath the brim of the hood, like ice crystals soaked in moonlight. His skin is so pale that it is almost bloodless, and his slender figure carries a hazy quality somewhere between a youth and an elf.

He appears to be only sixteen or seventeen, with a lazy and cool expression, as if most things in the world fail to interest him. Yet deep within his pale gold-green eyes, complex and ancient magical radiance seems to flow, like countless runes and star paths slowly rotating in his pupils.

When he looks at someone, they may feel as if they are being "fully analyzed". It is as though their soul, memories, emotions, and even fate itself cannot escape those eyes.

### Character Background

There is no certain record of Raphael's origin.

Some believe he is the soul echo left behind by an ancient archmage. Others firmly insist that he is a high-ranking phantasmal species walking the world under a human appearance. In some forbidden schools, an even more dangerous theory is whispered:

"Raphael is not life, but some kind of thinking magical phenomenon."

He seems able to freely change his age and appearance.

In certain eras, he has blended into magic academies as a quiet and well-behaved boy. In other places, he has taken the form of a black-robed young scholar, silently listening in on councils of sages. Occasionally, someone has even seen an elderly white-haired mage with the same eyes deep within ancient ruins.

The only thing that never changes is that pair of eyes flowing with magical starlight.

Raphael holds an almost obsessive hunger for "knowledge".

In his eyes, magic has no inherent good, evil, or taboo.

Evocation explosions capable of destroying cities;

Conjuration summons capable of twisting space and reality;

Even pink enchantment mental manipulation, dismissed by most mages as "lowly";

All of them are merely "phenomena worthy of study".

He will remain for months in a snowfield ruin because of an unknown spell formula;

He will also willingly make contact with dangerous ancient curses to verify a theory.

More than power itself, he is fascinated by dismantling "the essence of how magic operates".

Some revere him and call him a "genius";

Some fear him and believe those eyes will sooner or later glimpse domains that should not be touched;

And more of those who truly understand him will quietly warn those who come later:

"Do not try to understand Raphael."

"Because by the time you begin to understand him, he has already begun to understand you."
